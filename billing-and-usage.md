# Billing and Usage

### How and when are payments processed?

Daisychain billing is usage-based, with charges processed after the end of each month.

* Invoices and receipts are sent when your account is charged, typically in the first two weeks of each month, covering the previous month’s usage and any applicable subscription fees.
* By default, we auto-charge the payment method on file and send both the invoice and receipt via email at that time.

### What payment methods do you accept?

We currently support:

* Automatic bank debit (via ACH)
* Credit and debit cards
* Manual ACH transfers (Preferred for large payments)

To update your payment method, email billing@daisychain.app or contact your Account Manager.

### Can I remove autopay?

If you'd rather pay invoices manually, email billing@daisychain.app to discuss the options.&#x20;

### Can I change the billing method used for Daisychain?

Absolutely, you can do this by logging into the [Billing Portal](https://billing.stripe.com/p/login/28o9ExbHU4ev13i4gg) (using primary email address that receives invoices) or by emailing billing@daisychain.app to schedule a call to change your billing information.

### Billing Contacts

You can have multiple billing contacts receive invoices and receipts. To update your billing contact(s), email billing@daisychain.app.

### What’s included in the invoice?

Your monthly invoice may include:

* **Messaging Usage:** Charges for outgoing texting messages.&#x20;
* **Platform Subscription Fee:** Monthly access to Daisychain’s core tools like [pathways.md](organizing/pathways.md "mention")and [automations](organizing/automations/ "mention"). Does not apply for clients on a "texting only" plan.&#x20;
* **Add-ons:** Custom URLs, concierge service, etc.&#x20;

### Questions?

Reach us at billing@daisychain.app. We’re happy to help.

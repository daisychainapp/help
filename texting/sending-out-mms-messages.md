# Sending out MMS messages

### **What is an MMS?**

MMS stands for "Multimedia Messaging Service," and it is used to send more complicated messages than SMS messages. MMS messages in Daisychain can include images, videos, and/or up to 1,600 characters of text.

### **How do I send out an MMS message?**

There are two ways to send MMS messages in Daisychain:

#### **Adding several SMS segments worth of text**

If you send a long text in Daisychain which uses multiple SMS segments, we will automatically convert that message to an MMS if it is more cost-effective for you.

#### **Adding Pictures or Videos**

In the message composition window, click the image icon and select your image or video file. &#x20;

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/mms-add-image-toolbar.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

When you select a video, Daisychain prepares it for delivery automatically, and then it attaches to your message just like an image. See [Video](#video) below for ideas and tips.

***

### Other questions

#### **How big can my image file be?**

Your image file should be 500kb or less. If you need to resize your image, you can try using an online tool like [Image Resizer](https://imageresizer.com/).

#### **What dimensions should I use?**

In order to keep the file size small and the image easily viewable on a mobile phone, we generally recommend:

* Make your images square
* Making your image no more than 500px wide

#### **Any other tips?**

* **Don't Use Transparent Backgrounds:** These can appear as solid, randomly-colored backgrounds in the message thread, causing the image to look different than intended.
* **Consider using Charms and GIFs:** engaging, personalized content may get a better response than just text or a stock photo.
* **Use High Color Contrast:** Make sure the foreground and background colors are clearly different to prevent the image from appearing blurry or monochromatic.
* **Don't Use Borders:** On some iOS devices, borders may be displayed off-center.

### Video

Video plays right in the text thread, so supporters watch it without tapping a link or opening a browser. That makes a text a good way to put your best content in front of the people who already know you.

#### **What can I use video for?**

* **Your TV or digital ad:** you've already paid to make it. A standard 30-second spot fits, so you can send it straight to supporters, volunteers and donors — and ask them to share it.
* **A message from the candidate or a leader:** a personal thank-you after a donation, a GOTV reminder, or an invitation to an event lands differently when it's someone's face and voice.
* **Volunteer recruitment and training:** show what a canvass or phone bank actually looks like before asking someone to sign up.
* **Event recaps:** a few seconds from a rally or community meeting brings the energy to people who couldn't be there.

#### **Can people text videos back?**

Yes — video goes both ways. When someone replies with a video (or a photo), it shows up in the conversation in your inbox and you can play it right there. Ask supporters to reply with a short clip about why they're involved, and you've got a stream of real testimonials to learn from and, with their permission, share.

#### **What kind of video can I send?**

Your video can be up to **30 seconds** long and up to **150MB**, in any common video format. You don't need to resize or convert anything: Daisychain automatically prepares your video so mobile carriers will deliver it, and you'll see a brief "Compressing video…" indicator while that happens.

Carriers limit how large a text message can be, so videos are delivered at phone-friendly quality rather than the full resolution you uploaded. A few things help your video look its best on a phone:

* **Feature people and voices.** Someone speaking to camera, or a narrated ad, comes through clearly.
* **Keep on-screen text big.** Large, bold captions stay readable; fine print and small graphics may not.
* **Favor steady shots.** Fast cuts and quick motion are harder to compress than calmer footage.
* **Put the key message up front.** Lead with your ask in the first few seconds, and repeat it in the text of the message.

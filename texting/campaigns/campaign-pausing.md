# Campaign pausing

You can pause a campaign on the [campaign report page](campaign-report.md) by scrolling to the bottom:

![](https://44727351.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/44727351/Screenshot%202024-10-20%20at%204-36-39%E2%80%AFPM-png.png)

You can also resume at the bottom of the page if you wish:

![](https://44727351.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/44727351/Screenshot%202024-10-20%20at%204-38-55%E2%80%AFPM-png.png)

{% hint style="info" %}
You may only pause and un-pause a campaign once.
{% endhint %}

### Auto-pausing:

If your campaign encounters poor deliverability, Daisychain will automatically pause your campaign, and you'll see an alert like this:

![](https://44727351.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/44727351/Screenshot%202024-10-20%20at%204-38-08%E2%80%AFPM-png.png)

{% hint style="warning" %}
If your campaign is auto-paused, we strongly recommend reaching out to help@daisychain.app so our team can help diagnose your deliverability issues.&#x20;
{% endhint %}

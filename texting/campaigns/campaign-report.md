# Campaign Report

_After you send a campaign in Daisychain, you will have access to a campaign report with info about your send._

After a broadcast campaign is sent in Daisychain, you will have access to an analytics report for that campaign.

&#x20;

At the top of the page is the **audience** you’ve sent to – any lists included or excluded are displayed here.

![](https://44727351.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/44727351/Screenshot%202024-06-27%20at%203-36-13%E2%80%AFPM-png.png)

Below that is your campaign’s report, which can contain the following stages:

* On the far left is the number of recipients in your audience.
* Next is the number of recipients who Daisychain has received confirmation for delivery.
* Next is the number of unique clicks if you sent out a campaign with a trackable link.
* Finally, Daisychain will list the the number of “conversions” if you’re sending a campaign integrated with [ActBlue](https://help.daisychain.app/actblue) or [Mobilize](https://help.daisychain.app/mobilize)

![](https://44727351.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/44727351/Screenshot%202024-06-27%20at%203-37-18%E2%80%AFPM-png.png)

### **Activity**

You can view the number of clicks on each trackable URL you sent out. You may also click these links to see exactly who clicked.

![](https://44727351.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/44727351/Screenshot%202024-06-27%20at%203-38-24%E2%80%AFPM-png.png)

Opt-outs are listed as a percentage of those who were delivered the message you sent. Common opt-out words, such as “stop” and “take me off this list” are included here.

![](https://44727351.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/44727351/Screenshot%202024-06-27%20at%203-38-41%E2%80%AFPM-png.png)

Replies to your messages that are not obvious opt-out requests are also listed here as a percentage of messages delivered.

![](https://44727351.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/44727351/Screenshot%202024-06-27%20at%203-38-54%E2%80%AFPM-png.png)

You can also export replies by clicking “Download CSV”.

![](https://44727351.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/44727351/Screenshot%202024-06-27%20at%203-39-12%E2%80%AFPM-png.png)

### **Deliverability**

The deliverability stats of each broadcast campaign you send are listed at the bottom of the broadcast stats page. You can read more about deliverability here. But below is a short summary of these stats.

![](https://44727351.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/44727351/Screenshot%202024-06-27%20at%203-42-06%E2%80%AFPM-png.png)

**Sending** messages are those which have been sent to carriers but which we have not received a delivery receipt for. It may take up to two hours for these receipts to be received.

**Delivered** messages are those which we have received a delivery receipt for.

**Undelivered** messages are those which we have confirmed have not been delivered.

&#x20;

You may export the complete delivery statistics for your campaign at the bottom of this page by clicking “Export CSV”.

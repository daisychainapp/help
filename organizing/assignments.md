# Assignments

Every person in your Daisychain account can be assigned to a single [User](../settings/users-and-roles.md).

Assignments can be created, viewed, filtered, and modified in multiple places:

* In the [Inbox](../texting/inbox.md)\
  ​
* In [Pathways](pathways.md)\
  ​
* In the [People](../managing-data/filtering-people.md) view

You can also create Assignments when building an [Automation](automations/).

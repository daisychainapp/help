# Getting Started

Here are the most common things most people do when setting up a brand new Daisychain account.

* [ ] [**Set up integrations.**](integrations/integrations-overview.md)\
  Integrations with other tools help ensure that people and actions can flow into Daisychain in real-time — which can give you a 360-degree view of your supporters and help speed up follow-up and increase engagement. \

* [ ] [**Import a list.**](managing-data/csv-imports.md) \
  Upload a CSV of your supporters so you can start your organizing and outreach campaigns — if you want to import to[ custom fields](managing-data/custom-fields.md) or [tags](managing-data/tags.md), make sure to create them before uploading your list. \

* [ ] [**Invite your team.**](settings/users-and-roles.md) \
  Whether it's other members of your staff or volunteer texters, Daisychain shines when you use it with a team.\

* [ ] [**Create a texting campaign.** ](texting/campaigns/)\
  When you're ready to begin your outreach, create a campaign to quickly send personalized, outbound text messages.&#x20;

Our team is always happy to help get you started, so email [help@daisychain.app](mailto:help@daisychain.app) if you have questions or want to setup an onboarding call.
